The Roof Ledger

BASELINE → CADENCE → FLAGGED Δ
CONDITION IS A FILE, NOT A SNAPSHOT

One scan is a data point. Jinki opens a condition-of-record at baseline and accrues it on cadence — so you watch a roof change in velocity, not discover it failed at the claim.

The file is version-controlled: baseline → on-cadence re-scans → post-event capture → a flagged Δ — the entry that reprices a renewal or documents a loss.
