UNDERWRITING & CLAIMS LENS

Unpriced roof condition costs on both desks — it mis-selects the book, and it litigates the claim.

Underwriting lens
  • Moderate-to-poor roofs carry ~60% higher loss cost.Verisk RCS, 2025
  • Aging (~20-year) roofs are materially likelier to claim — practitioner estimate, directional, wide range.
  • Condition priced by age, not seen, is adverse selection you can't observe.
Claims lens
  • Loss-adjustment expense runs ~10–15% of losses paid.IRMI
  • Disputes concentrate: FL has generated a large majority (~70%+) of US homeowners-insurance litigation while representing a small share of claims.III / FL OIR, ~2021–22
  • A thin file is what turns a covered loss into a contested one.
