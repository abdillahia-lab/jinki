02 · PROBLEM

Roof is the leading loss driver — and the most-disputed line

ASSESSED LATE · INDEFENSIBLY

The roof drives the loss and drives the dispute — yet its condition is rarely on file in a form that holds up.

~40–43% of homeowners losses are wind & hail — the #1 driver, and roof-first. III, 2023–24
~25% of residential claim value is roof-related — roughly $31B of 2024 costs. Verisk, 2024
~38% of US roofs read moderate-to-poor in aerial condition data. Verisk, 2025

Roof condition is assessed late, inconsistently, or in a form that doesn't hold up — so a thin file becomes a disputed claim.
